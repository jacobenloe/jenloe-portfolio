Measuring liquid surface tension

Surface tension is the force at the surface of a liquid. I measured it with a laser, using standing-wave behavior and diffraction. It was a lab project at CU Boulder, with Brooks Kioschos, dated May 1, 2025.

Ultrasonic testing of lithium-ion batteries

A review paper on using ultrasound to find defects inside lithium-ion batteries. Time of flight tracks state of health. It cannot yet be shown to work for all battery sizes and shapes.
